The product

Meet the portable solar charger.

A solution designed for people who need power even far from outlets. The portable solar charger uses photovoltaic panels to convert sunlight into electricity and charge compatible devices.

Charging performance depends on sunlight intensity, panel capacity and the device's specifications.

HÉLIOS engineering

Inside HÉLIOS

Smart engineering. Energy that comes from the Sun.

HÉLIOS combines solar energy, energy storage and smart monitoring in a portable charger designed to offer more autonomy and convenience. Its concept uses affordable electronic components, with the potential to evolve into a commercial solution.

How the energy flows

Step 1 of 6

Solar panel

Turns sunlight into electrical energy.

Auxiliary monitoring system — does not supply power to the phone

The sensor measures the circuit, the ESP32 reads the data and the display presents it. The energy that reaches the phone passes only through the main chain: panel, controller, battery, converter and USB-C output.

Components used

Each module has a clear function. All items below are design references, subject to electrical compatibility validation.

Monitoring

ESP32 DevKit V1

Microcontroller responsible for smart monitoring.

Possibilities

  • Receive information from the sensors.
  • Monitor voltage and current in compatible circuits.
  • Send information to an OLED display.
  • Enable future connectivity features.

Programming: C++ with the Arduino IDE.

Energy

Photovoltaic solar panel

Converts sunlight into electrical energy.

Initial reference

Compact 6 V, 3–5 W module, subject to compatibility validation with the power management circuit.

Performance depends on lighting conditions, and the panel's power must be sized according to the product's purpose.

Energy

Solar charge controller

Manages the energy produced by the panel and charges the battery appropriately.

The circuit must be compatible with the panel and with the battery's chemistry, voltage and configuration.

Energy

Protected battery

Stores energy to allow phone charging even when no sunlight is available.

Battery pack with appropriate management and protection.

Capacity has not yet been defined.

Energy

Voltage converter and USB output

Provides regulated power for the USB output.

The output must be compatible with the battery, the required current and the specifications of the connected device. Fast charging is not promised: it would depend on implementing and validating the necessary protocols.

Monitoring

INA219 sensor

Measures voltage and current in a compatible circuit, allowing the ESP32 to obtain operating information.

Monitoring

0.96-inch OLED display

Shows information such as the HÉLIOS name, measured voltage, measured current and system status.

Technical note: safety and limitations

  • A real assembly requires compatibility between the panel, controller, battery and USB output.
  • Do not connect the solar panel directly to a lithium battery or to the phone. The assembly must have appropriate management and protection, and assembly and testing must be supervised by someone with electronics experience.
  • HÉLIOS is still a prototype proposal, not a certified or commercially validated product.
